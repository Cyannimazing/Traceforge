# TraceForge brand assets

beetle-concept.png was generated for this project using the built-in image generation tool on 2026-11-01. Transparent background requested. Source output retained in the Codex generated_images directory.

Prompt: Production brand mascot for TraceForge Engine, professional blockchain investigation tool. A striking premium 3D mechanical beetle viewed from above at a slight isometric angle, sculpted compact dark titanium shield shell, beautiful silver bevels, unmistakable capital T in muted teal inset into the shell, six articulated mechanical legs and two small antennae. Strong dimensional industrial product render, realistic metallic lighting, precision machining, sophisticated not cartoonish. Entire insect isolated centered on truly transparent background, no floor or shadow backdrop, no text except the geometric T on shell, no additional objects, generous margins, square composition. Clean recognizable silhouette suitable as app emblem.

traceforge-icon.svg and traceforge-logo.svg are independently authored geometric vector companion assets. The animated mark uses the same six-leg shield/T geometry. No SwarmEngine artwork or source code is bundled.


## Replacement identity
The insect concept was retired at the user's request. Active mark: custom authored geometric TF monogram with cut corners, solid off-white and muted green. No gradients. The wordmark and favicon assets now use this monogram. The earlier generated beetle PNG is retained only as a historical concept asset and is not referenced by the interface.
