# SwarmEngine design reconstruction

Date: 2026-11-02. Mode: Operate. User explicitly selected SwarmEngine's public design as the reference and deferred historical-provider work. The existing reference audit and fresh homepage/report inspection govern this pass; no new aesthetic-choice interview is necessary.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make investigation visible as a sequence of evidence checks, followed by a network and a plain-language decision.

OWN-WORLD: Black ground, charcoal node headers, fine gray rules, white selected controls, squared panels. Space Grotesk for the product and explanation; JetBrains Mono for controls, addresses and measurements. White/gray visual hierarchy follows the reference. Written warning states retain meaning independently of color.

STORY: Paste an address, watch actual stages, inspect the verdict, select a wallet, read supporting evidence. A clearly labeled example explains interactions without inventing live activity.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 62px top navigation bar and centered 1240px console. Framed title and scan form above a node/fiber engine. After a report, a collapsed search gives room to token details, market metrics, map and assessment, then a visible holder workspace. On phones the verdict precedes the map.

FORM: User-assigned SwarmEngine public reference. Independent React/SVG/CSS implementation. No random style selection. Original source evidence was retained in the prior REA audit; screenshots and current public behavior were rechecked. No private backend or service integration is claimed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Boundaries

- Existing engine, APIs, classification rules, caches and credentials are unchanged.
- No synthetic safety score, fake activity counters, unrelated Swarm branding or service credentials.
- No 3D assets or roaming insect are reintroduced.
- Graph displays a bounded sample and documented edges, not beneficial ownership.
- Actual scan events drive motion; reduced-motion, hidden-document and offscreen guards apply.
- Fonts are self-hosted from Google Fonts with their OFL license files.

## Verification plan

Typecheck and existing tests; production build; desktop/phone visual inspection; keyboard chain selection; report/table/map/drawer selection; navigation and zoom controls; console errors and page overflow; final production screenshot.

## Motion refinement

The ready instrument uses two counter-rotating arcs, six staggered signal traces, and a bounded field of decorative points. They do not represent measured transactions. Hover and keyboard focus highlight the corresponding pipeline route; real scan stages increase route emphasis. Dropdowns open in 180ms and evidence panels use the existing GSAP 220ms transition. Canvas is capped at 40 points, 30fps and DPR1.5. Offscreen, hidden-tab and OS reduced-motion guards remain; no user-visible motion toggle is added. Dropdown menu width equals its trigger at desktop and phone widths; all four rows are62px.
