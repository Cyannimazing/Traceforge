# TraceForge Engine
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
Web research app, desktop and mobile.
## Stack
Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, authored CSS, GSAP for brief state transitions, server-only provider adapters.
## Purpose
Explain who holds a token, who controls its contract, where wallet funding came from, and what evidence suggests related buying or selling.
## Users and workflow
Users need plain-language guidance rather than a trading terminal. Paste an address, read the verdict, inspect the immediately visible holder table, filter wallet roles, then open supporting evidence. Creator and current owner are separate roles. Technical detail is disclosed after selection.
## Current design requirements
User-assigned SwarmEngine public reference: black instrument console, compact top navigation, squared panels, evidence-stage diagram, white controls, Space Grotesk and JetBrains Mono. Report network and verdict lead into an accessible holder table and evidence drawer. No 3D models or roaming insect. Ordinary hyphens. This 2026-11-02 pass supersedes the previous teal sidebar direction. Missing data remains explicit.
## Data boundaries
TraceForge computes the default verdict independently from public market/security data and configured Alchemy historical transactions. SwarmEngine is not called by the primary scan or Discovery assessment endpoint. SwarmEngine claims retain per-record attribution, original scan time and retrieval time; imported reports do not establish canonical launch completeness. Facts, inferences, unavailable checks and sample limits must stay separate. No wallet connection, trading, synthetic risk probability, token issuance, or implied recovery of SwarmEngine's private backend.
